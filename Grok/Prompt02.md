**Fractional CTOs do not typically receive a traditional salary.** They are independent contractors or consultants paid via hourly rates, monthly retainers, or project fees rather than as employees (no benefits, payroll taxes, or standard equity packages in most cases).

### Typical 2026 Compensation Ranges (Primarily US Market)
Data from industry benchmarks, surveys, and marketplaces shows these common figures:

| Pricing Model              | Typical Range                          | Common Scope                          | Notes |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|-------|
| **Hourly rate**           | $150–$500/hr (most common $200–$400/hr; average ~$217/hr) | Advisory, spot decisions, due diligence | Higher end for AI/fintech/healthtech specialists or top-tier experience; lower for less experienced or non-US talent. |
| **Monthly retainer**      | $5,000–$25,000/month (most common $8,000–$15,000–$18,000) | 5–20 hours/week (or 1–3 days/week)   | Median around $15,000 for ~2 days/week. Advisory (lighter) often $2k–$10k; heavier/embedded $15k–$30k+. |
| **Annualized equivalent** | $60,000–$180,000+ (for standard part-time engagements) | Ongoing leadership                  | Significantly lower than a full-time CTO’s fully loaded cost ($250k–$400k+ salary + benefits + equity + recruiting). |
| **Project-based**         | $10,000–$60,000 per engagement        | Audits, roadmaps, migrations         | Fixed-scope work. |

### Key Factors Affecting Rates
- **Experience and seniority** — Mid-level (10–18 years): lower end; senior/principal (ex-full-time CTO at scaled or VC-backed companies, 18–25+ years): higher end ($275–$600+/hr or $12k–$30k+/month).
- **Time commitment** — Advisory (a few hours/week) is cheapest; active/embedded (15–25+ hours/week) costs more.
- **Location** — Highest in SF/NYC/Seattle ($275–$350+/hr); moderate in other US metros; lower in UK/Europe, Eastern Europe, LATAM, or APAC.
- **Specialization** — AI/ML, fintech, healthtech, security, or regulated industries often command 20–40% premiums.
- **Company stage** — Pre-seed/seed: often lighter retainers ($5k–$12k); Series A+: higher for more hands-on work.
- **Equity** — Most prefer (or require) cash. Some accept small equity (e.g., 0.25–1.5%) as a kicker, especially at early-stage startups, but pure equity-only deals are frequently declined.

### Comparison to Full-Time CTO
A full-time CTO typically costs $250,000–$400,000+ in base salary alone, plus benefits (20–40% overhead), equity (0.5–3%+), recruiting fees, and severance risk—often totaling $400k–$600k+ in year one. A fractional arrangement delivers much of the strategic value at roughly 20–40% of that cost with greater flexibility.

Rates vary by individual negotiations, exact scope, and market conditions. For the most current or location-specific figures, check recent marketplace data (e.g., Go Fractional) or speak directly with practitioners.
