### Other Useful Sites & Approaches
- **Specialized fractional job boards** — Sites like HireAFractionalExec.com or GotFractional.com aggregate Fractional CTO, interim, and advisory roles from multiple sources.
- **Broader marketplaces** — Upwork or Arc.dev can surface Fractional CTO-style opportunities (filter carefully for senior/strategic roles). Quality varies more than on dedicated platforms.
- **LinkedIn** — Still one of the highest-volume sources. Search for “Fractional CTO,” “Part-time CTO,” “Interim CTO,” or “CTO as a Service,” set alerts, and network with founders, VCs, and other fractional executives.
- **Niche or firm-specific networks** — CTOx (CTO-focused firm), TechCXO (more enterprise/PE-oriented), or Catalant (enterprise projects).

### Tips for Success
Most high-quality Fractional CTO roles come through a mix of these platforms **plus** personal networks, referrals from past colleagues/investors, and outbound outreach to startups or PE/VC portfolio companies. Create a strong profile highlighting prior CTO/VP Engineering experience, specific outcomes (scaling teams, architecture decisions, fundraising diligence, etc.), and available capacity (e.g., 10–20 hours/week).

Rates and demand fluctuate, so check live listings on the platforms above for the most current opportunities. Starting with Go Fractional and Fractional Jobs tends to yield the best combination of volume and relevance for most candidates.
