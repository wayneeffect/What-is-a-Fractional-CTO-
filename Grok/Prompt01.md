**A Fractional CTO (or fCTO) is a senior technology executive who provides part-time, high-level Chief Technology Officer services to one or more companies on a flexible, contract, or retainer basis.**

The “fractional” aspect means the company receives a portion of the person’s time (typically 1–4 days per week, or roughly 5–25 hours), not a reduced level of expertise or responsibility. They deliver the same strategic leadership as a full-time CTO—technology strategy, architecture decisions, engineering team guidance, roadmaps, and executive-level reporting—but without the full-time salary, benefits, equity, or long-term employment commitment.

### Key Characteristics
- **Part-time and often multi-client**: They commonly serve several organizations at once, bringing cross-company experience.
- **Embedded and outcome-oriented**: Unlike pure consultants (who often deliver recommendations and leave), a strong fractional CTO joins meetings, reviews work, makes decisions, owns results, and acts as an extension of the leadership team.
- **Flexible engagement**: Usually ongoing for months to a couple of years (e.g., 6–18 months), with the ability to scale hours up or down. It is distinct from an interim CTO (temporary full-time coverage during a transition).

### Typical Responsibilities
- Defining technology strategy and aligning it with business goals
- Creating or refining technical roadmaps
- Guiding architecture, scalability, and technical decisions
- Overseeing or advising engineering teams (hiring, processes, culture)
- Vendor evaluation, build-vs-buy decisions, and technical due diligence
- Risk, security, and compliance considerations
- Board- or investor-level technology reporting

They focus more on strategy, prioritization, and leadership than day-to-day coding.

### Who Uses Them and Why
Fractional CTOs are popular with startups (especially pre-seed to Series A), scale-ups, and small-to-medium businesses that need executive tech leadership but do not yet have enough work, budget, or complexity to justify a full-time hire (often $250k–$400k+ annually plus equity and benefits). Costs for fractional arrangements commonly range from roughly $5k–$25k per month depending on scope, experience, and hours.

In short, it is a cost-effective way to access seasoned CTO-level judgment and ownership when a permanent, full-time executive is not yet necessary or affordable.
